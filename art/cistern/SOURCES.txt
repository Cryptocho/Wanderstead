Wanderstead / Copper Kettle — original cistern art

Sources
- Geometry, UV layouts, actual beveled masonry, fluted octagonal columns, raised aqueduct troughs, spring-keeper effigy, folded cloth, foliage, coffers, handwheel and pressure lids: original mesh artwork authored for this project in tools/make_cistern.py; Blender 4.5 LTS (bpy).
- Active textures: original deterministic 32/64-pixel drawings in tools/make_cistern_textures.py. Cool limestone strata, stepped water-leaf carving, ewer-and-stream heraldry, walnut joinery, oxidized bronze and broken mosaic were drawn specifically for this sanctuary. No photographic material input or natural-photo noise is used.
- The same tool creates independent sculpted-height tangent normals, roughness maps and metallic masks. Bronze oxidation is less metallic and rougher than exposed metal. All four material channels are embedded in cistern.glb; textures use nearest filtering and export includes explicit tangents.
- source/spring_material_atlas.png and source/ornament_material_atlas.png are archived, superseded OpenAI gpt-image-2 outputs from 2026-11-22. They are no longer inputs to either authoring script.
- User-provided Elin references, the earlier ruin.png and reference/visual/spring-cistern-study.webp informed scale, readability and atmosphere only. No pixels from those references are included in the assets or previews.

Regeneration
python tools/make_cistern_textures.py
blender-4.5 --factory-startup --background -t 4 --python tools/make_cistern.py

Integration
Authoritative collision and placement source: assets/maps/cistern.grid, 21x15, world x=-10..10, z=-7..7. Tile top y=0; one cell=one metre. Map, portals, pressure-cover positions, coffer positions and hinge transforms are unchanged. Dynamic water belongs to the engine at y=-0.22. GLB exports before preview-only water, lights and camera are created. The editable cistern.blend includes the physical preview water and north-facing inspection rig; PNGs are actual Blender renders, not game screenshots or composited scenery.
The three independently animated roots remain tool_lid and tonic_lid (local X rotation -1.7 opens), and wheel_pickup (hide root and descendants when acquired). Mesh wheel_pickup_bronze remains independently parented. Wet paving and Ivory mosaic material names remain compatible with runtime mapping.
The former amethyst feature is now pale blue/emerald spring fluorite with faceted luminous cleavages. Legacy material and light names remain for integration compatibility. east_amethyst retains position [8.82,1.55,0.08]; recommended light RGB is [0.30,0.75,0.87]. All other lamp anchors and water heights are unchanged.
Exact anchors, PBR/shader hints and mesh budget are emitted to integration.json. Engine effect anchors remain assets/cistern/lights.json. authored_pixel_materials.png is the original texture contact sheet. preview_north_overview.png, preview_north_near.png, preview_east_near.png, preview_relic_eye.png, preview_fluorite.png and preview_coffers_open.png inspect the complete architecture, near/eye-level effigy, faceted mineral and actual opened hinged lids.
